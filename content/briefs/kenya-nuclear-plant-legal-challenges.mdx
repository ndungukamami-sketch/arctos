## Bottom line

A petition in the Environment and Land Court in Kisumu, filed on 1 September and certified urgent on 7 September, asks the court to stop any irreversible construction works on the proposed Sh500 billion plant at Lenya village, Siaya County, until the government shows it met constitutional, statutory, environmental, public participation, land and financing requirements. A separate reference before the East African Court of Justice challenges the project from a regional angle, and reports describe domestic cases over Kilifi (Uyombo) as well. The Kenya Nuclear Regulatory Authority is a respondent. No vendor, loan or intergovernmental agreement is named in the sources found, so the contractual side of the project is not yet visible.

## The parties and the instrument

The petitioner in Kisumu is Francis Owino. He raises risks to Lake Victoria, radioactive waste and public finances, and invokes the precautionary principle. The regional reference is reported by IntelliNews, Mongabay and Kenyans.co.ke as raising environmental and treaty questions under the East African Community framework.

## The constitutional and treaty backdrop

Article 42 of the Constitution guarantees a clean and healthy environment, and Article 69 sets duties on natural resources including public participation. The East African Community Treaty sets shared obligations on the Lake Victoria basin. How those apply is for the courts, and this brief does not predict it.

## What the record shows

- Kisumu ELC petition: filed 1 September, certified urgent 7 September.

## The power reading

A project of this size usually rests on a vendor country's financing and a state to state agreement. If one exists, it is the instrument that fixes Kenya's liability and fuel and waste arrangements, and it is the document Arctos should seek first.

## What to watch

- Any order halting works from the Kisumu court.
- Publication of the vendor, financing and any intergovernmental agreement.
- The East African Court of Justice's admission decision on the reference.
